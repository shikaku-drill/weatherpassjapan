Fine in the rain — indoor

  • 🍵Tea Ceremony in a Kimono
    Higashiyama, Kyoto · approx. ¥5,200 · 75 min
    Matcha whisked in front of you in a quiet tatami room — kimono included.
  • 🦎Kyoto Aquarium
    Umekoji, Kyoto · approx. ¥2,300 · 90 min
    Giant salamanders and dolphins, a 15-minute walk from Kyoto Station.
  • 🌸Kyoto Wagashi Sweets Workshop
    Downtown Kyoto · approx. ¥3,100 · 60 min
    Shape delicate seasonal sweets by hand, then enjoy them with matcha.
  • 🤼Kyoto Sumo Show & Chanko Experience
    Shimogyo, Kyoto · approx. ¥8,900 · 90 min
    Retired wrestlers demo classic moves indoors — try a bout yourself, then share a chanko hot pot.
  • 🥷Ninja & Samurai Dress-up + Combat Class
    Nakagyo, Kyoto · approx. ¥4,600 · 90 min
    Suit up in armor or ninja garb, learn sword choreography, and pose for photo-worthy action shots.
  • 🎨Kyo-Yuzen & Fan-Making Workshop
    Nakagyo, Kyoto · approx. ¥3,300 · 90 min
    Hand-dye a silk scarf or paint a folding fan with a Kyoto artisan — a wearable souvenir you make yourself.
  • 💍Vintage Coin Ring Workshop
    Nakagyo, Kyoto · approx. ¥5,800 · 120 min
    Hammer a real vintage coin into a one-of-a-kind ring under a craftsman's guidance — a unique Kyoto keepsake.
  • 🍵Uji Matcha Grinding & Tasting
    Uji, Kyoto · approx. ¥2,600 · 90 min
    Grind your own matcha on a stone mill in Japan's tea capital, then taste it with a wagashi sweet.
  • 🏯Sanjusangendo Hall
    Higashiyama, Kyoto · pay at the gate · 50 min
    1,001 gilded Kannon statues standing in a 120m wooden hall. Entirely indoor, and unlike anything else on earth.
  • 🚂Kyoto Railway Museum
    Umekoji, Kyoto · approx. ¥1,500 · closed Wednesdays · 150 min
    53 real locomotives including a working steam roundhouse you can ride from.
  • 🏺Kyoto National Museum
    Higashiyama, Kyoto · approx. ¥700 · 90 min
    Meiji-era brick halls holding Heian sculpture, sutras and tea ware.
  • 📚Kyoto International Manga Museum
    Karasuma, Kyoto · approx. ¥1,200 · 120 min
    50,000 volumes on open shelves in an old school — sit on the lawn and read all day.
  • 🍲Soba-Making Class
    Central Kyoto · approx. ¥6,000 · 120 min
    Knead, roll and cut buckwheat by hand, then eat it cold with dipping sauce.
  • 🎎Maiko Performance & Dinner
    Gion, Kyoto · approx. ¥13,000 · 120 min
    Dance, conversation through an interpreter and the drinking games — the real ozashiki, arranged for visitors.
  • 🧘Zazen Meditation at a Temple
    Higashiyama temples · approx. ¥3,000 · 90 min
    Forty minutes of sitting, a monk's talk, and the stick if your posture slips.
  • 📄Washi Paper-Making Class
    Central Kyoto · approx. ¥3,800 · 90 min
    Scoop the pulp, press the sheet, add pressed flowers — dried and ready before you leave.
  • 🍱Kaiseki Dinner in a Machiya
    Gion / Pontocho · approx. ¥18,000 · 150 min
    Eight or nine seasonal courses in a wooden townhouse, each dish explained in English.
  • 🍵Tea Ceremony in a Tatami Room
    Central Kyoto · approx. ¥4,500 · 75 min
    Sit in a real tea room, learn the bow and the turn of the bowl, then whisk your own.
  • 🏺Kiyomizu-yaki Pottery Painting
    Higashiyama, Kyoto · approx. ¥4,500 · 120 min
    Paint a cup or plate in the kiln district below Kiyomizu-dera; fired and posted to you.
  • 🍶Fushimi Sake Brewery Tour & Tasting
    Fushimi, Kyoto · approx. ¥3,500 · 90 min
    Kyoto's brewing quarter, where the soft water makes a rounder sake than Nada's.
  • 🥢Kyoto Vegetarian (Shojin) Cooking Class
    Central Kyoto · approx. ¥7,000 · 120 min
    Temple cooking with no meat or fish — dashi from kombu, yuba, and Kyoto's own vegetables.
  • 🧶Kumihimo Braiding Workshop
    Central Kyoto · approx. ¥4,000 · 90 min
    Silk braiding on a wooden stand — the cords that tie kimono, made into a bracelet.
  • 🕯Incense Blending (Kodo)
    Central Kyoto · approx. ¥4,500 · 90 min
    Blend sandalwood and spices to your own recipe, and learn how to 'listen' to incense.
  • 🥷Samurai & Ninja Museum with Experience
    Central Kyoto · approx. ¥5,500 · 120 min
    Armour to try on, a guided tour in English, then shuriken and sword practice.

Mostly covered

  • 🥢Nishiki Market Food Tour

Best under blue sky

  • 🎋Arashiyama Rickshaw Ride
    Arashiyama, Kyoto · approx. ¥4,800 · 60 min
    A rickshaw runner tours you through the bamboo grove.
  • ⛩️Fushimi Inari & Nara Day Tour
    Departs Kyoto Station · approx. ¥10,900 · 540 min
    Thousand torii gates, then Nara's bowing deer — one guided day.
  • 🌲Amanohashidate & Ine Bay Day Tour
    Departs Osaka / Kyoto · approx. ¥11,400 · 600 min
    One of Japan's 'three great views' — a pine-clad sandbar, plus the boat-house village nicknamed the Venice of Japan.
  • 🚡Biwako Valley Ropeway & Terrace
    Otsu, Shiga · approx. ¥2,900 · 180 min
    Japan's fastest ropeway climbs to a cliff-edge terrace floating above Lake Biwa — the country's largest lake.
  • 🪷Uji Byodo-in & Matcha Village Tour
    Departs Kyoto · approx. ¥6,500 · 420 min
    The Phoenix Hall on the 10-yen coin, wrapped in a stroll through Uji's centuries-old matcha teahouses.
  • 🏯Nijo Castle
    Nakagyo, Kyoto · pay at the gate · 90 min
    The shogun's Kyoto palace with 'nightingale' floors that chirp underfoot, and a strolling garden.
  • 🏯Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)
    Kita, Kyoto · pay at the gate · 60 min
    Gold leaf over three floors, mirrored in the pond. Pay at the gate; the ticket is a paper charm.
  • 🏯Ginkaku-ji (Silver Pavilion)
    Sakyo, Kyoto · pay at the gate · 60 min
    Raked sand cones and a moss garden climbing the hillside — restraint where Kinkaku-ji is excess.
  • 🛕To-ji Temple & Five-Storey Pagoda
    South Kyoto · approx. ¥500 · 60 min
    Japan's tallest wooden pagoda, five minutes from Kyoto Station.
  • 🪨Ryoan-ji Rock Garden
    Northwest Kyoto · approx. ¥600 · 50 min
    The most famous dry garden in Japan — fifteen stones, never all visible at once.
  • 🚲Kyoto Backstreets Cycling Tour
    From Kyoto Station · approx. ¥6,500 · 180 min
    Flat city, narrow lanes, a guide who knows which temples are empty at 9am.
  • 🛶Hozugawa River Boat Ride
    Kameoka → Arashiyama · approx. ¥4,500 · 120 min
    Two hours down a gorge in a wooden boat, poled by three boatmen through the rapids.
  • 🏮Gion Night Walking Tour
    Gion, Kyoto · approx. ¥4,500 · 120 min
    The lanes after the day-trippers leave, with the etiquette explained so you don't intrude.
