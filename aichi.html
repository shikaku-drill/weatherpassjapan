What to do in Aichi when it rains

43 places across Aichi, with 13 that work in the rain. Pick by the weather you actually have — the live app sorts them by how long it takes to get there from where you stand.

Fine in the rain — indoor

  • 🚄SCMAGLEV and Railway Park
    Kinjofuto, Nagoya · approx. ¥950 · 120 min
    Real shinkansen and maglev trains you can board, plus a driving simulator.
  • 🐋Port of Nagoya Public Aquarium
    Minato, Nagoya · approx. ¥1,900 · 150 min
    Home to orcas, belugas and a vast dolphin pool — one of Japan's biggest aquariums, a rainy-day winner in Nagoya.
  • 🧵Toyota Commemorative Museum
    Nagoya, Aichi · pay at the gate · closed Mondays · 120 min
    From looms to robots — Toyota's origin story with live machine demos, great on a rainy day.
  • 🗡Tokugawa Art Museum
    Ozone, Nagoya · approx. ¥1,600 · closed Mondays · 90 min
    Samurai armour, swords and the Genji Monogatari scrolls, from the Owari Tokugawa house.
  • 🚗Toyota Automobile Museum
    Nagakute, Aichi · approx. ¥1,200 · 120 min
    140 cars tracing automotive history worldwide, not just Toyota.
  • 🪐Nagoya City Science Museum
    Fushimi, Nagoya · approx. ¥800 · 120 min
    One of the world's largest planetarium domes, inside a giant silver globe.
  • 🍽Noritake Craft Center
    Noritake Garden, Nagoya · approx. ¥500 · 90 min
    Watch bone china being shaped and painted, then try painting a plate yourself.
  • 🧱INAX Live Museum
    Tokoname, Aichi · approx. ¥700 · 120 min
    A tile and ceramics museum where you can decorate your own tiles by hand.
  • 🐬Minami-Chita Beachland Aquarium
    Minamichita, Aichi · approx. ¥1,900 · 120 min
    A hands-on aquarium where you can touch dolphins and feed the seals.
  • 🥯Nagoya Anpanman Children's Museum
    Minato, Nagoya · approx. ¥2,000 · 150 min
    Indoor play park built around the Anpanman characters — made for small children on a rainy day.
  • 🏺Tokoname Pottery Wheel Class
    Tokoname, Aichi · approx. ¥3,500 · 90 min
    One of Japan's six ancient kiln towns — throw a cup and have it fired and shipped.
  • 🍲Hatcho Miso Brewery Tour & Tasting
    Okazaki, Aichi · approx. ¥4,000 · 90 min
    Two-year-old cedar vats weighted with river stones, then miso soup made three ways.
  • 🍜Nagoya Kishimen & Miso-Katsu Class
    Nagoya Station area · approx. ¥5,000 · 100 min
    Roll the flat noodles by hand and fry the cutlet, then eat both with the local red miso.

Best under blue sky

  • 🌰Ghibli Park
    Nagakute, Aichi · approx. ¥3,700 · 300 min
    Walk into Ghibli's worlds. Pavilions are indoors but sit far apart across a large park — expect long outdoor walks; tickets are book-ahead, date-and-time specific.
  • 🧱LEGOLAND Japan (Nagoya)
    Minato, Nagoya · approx. ¥4,300 · 300 min
    An outdoor theme park built for 2–12s — rides, driving school and millions of LEGO bricks by Nagoya's bay.
  • ⛩Atsuta Shrine
    Nagoya, Aichi · pay at the gate · 90 min
    A 1,900-year-old shrine in a cedar grove, keeper of the sacred sword — Nagoya's spiritual heart.
  • 🗼Mirai Tower & Oasis 21
    Sakae, Nagoya · approx. ¥800 · 60 min
    Japan's oldest TV tower, now a night-view deck above the glass 'water spaceship' of Oasis 21.
  • 🏯Inuyama Castle & Old Town
    Inuyama, Aichi · pay at the gate · 120 min
    One of only twelve original keeps, with a river view — and a snack-lined castle town below.
  • 🦍Higashiyama Zoo & Botanical Gardens
    Higashiyama, Nagoya · approx. ¥500 · 180 min
    One of Japan's biggest zoos — the famous gorilla Shabani, plus a greenhouse and botanical garden.
  • 🎡Laguna Ten Bosch
    Gamagori, Aichi · approx. ¥2,250 · 240 min
    Seaside theme park with rides, pools and evening illumination on Mikawa Bay.
  • 🏛Museum Meiji-Mura
    Inuyama, Aichi · approx. ¥2,000 · 240 min
    Over 60 relocated Meiji-era buildings across a huge hillside park, including Frank Lloyd Wright's Imperial Hotel lobby.
  • 🌍Little World Museum of Man
    Inuyama, Aichi · approx. ¥1,800 · 210 min
    Walk through full-scale houses from around the world and try on national costumes.
  • 🏯Okazaki Castle
    Okazaki, Aichi · approx. ¥300 · 90 min
    Birthplace of Tokugawa Ieyasu, with a museum tower in a cherry-blossom park.
  • 🦁Nonhoi Park (Toyohashi Zoo)
    Toyohashi, Aichi · approx. ¥600 · 180 min
    Zoo, botanical garden, amusement rides and a natural-history museum in one park.
  • 🌰Ghibli Park Big Ghibli Warehouse Pass
    Nagakute, Aichi · approx. ¥7,300 · 300 min
    The indoor warehouse area — film sets to stand in, and it works in any weather.
