Fine in the rain — indoor

  • 🦈Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan
    Osaka Bay · approx. ¥2,600 · 120 min
    Whale sharks circling one of the world's largest tanks, eight floors deep.
  • 🏙Abeno Harukas 300 Observatory
    Tennoji, Osaka · approx. ¥2,000 · 75 min
    Japan's tallest skyscraper — a 300m glass observatory over the whole Kansai plain.
  • 🗼Tsutenkaku Tower
    Shinsekai, Osaka · approx. ¥900 · 60 min
    Osaka's retro symbol tower, with the lucky god Billiken and a glass-floor sky walk.
  • 🧱Legoland Discovery Center Osaka
    Tempozan, Osaka · approx. ¥2,400 · 180 min
    Indoor Lego play world beside the harbour — rides, a 4D cinema and Miniland Osaka.
  • 🍜Cupnoodles Museum Osaka Ikeda
    Ikeda, Osaka · approx. ¥500 · 90 min
    Make your own original cup noodle by hand — the birthplace of instant ramen.
  • 🐧Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan (evening)
    Tempozan, Osaka · approx. ¥2,200 · 90 min
    The whale-shark tank after dark, when the exhibits shift to a calmer night mood.
  • 🐙Takoyaki & Okonomiyaki Class
    Dotonbori, Osaka · approx. ¥4,300 · 90 min
    Flip your own takoyaki like a local in the heart of Dotonbori.
  • 🛁Spa World Osaka
    Shinsekai, Osaka · approx. ¥1,400 · 180 min
    Bathe your way around the world — Roman, Balinese and Finnish baths under one roof.
  • 🏓Round1 Spo-Cha
    Sennichimae, Osaka · approx. ¥2,800 · 180 min
    Unlimited bowling, karaoke, batting cages and arcade — all night if you want.
  • 🎮VS PARK Umeda (active games)
    Kita, Osaka · approx. ¥2,500 · 120 min
    Dozens of body-moving mini-games indoors — sprint from a fake lion, hit buzzers, rack up scores with friends.
  • 🦩NIFREL Living Museum (EXPOCITY)
    Suita, Osaka · approx. ¥2,100 · 120 min
    Part aquarium, part zoo, part art — walk among free-roaming penguins and white tigers under artful lighting.
  • 🏛Osaka Museum of History
    Chuo, Osaka · pay at the gate · 90 min
    Ten floors of Osaka's past with a picture-window view straight onto the castle — dry all day.
  • 🏘Osaka Museum of Housing and Living
    Tenjinbashi, Osaka · approx. ¥600 · 90 min
    A full-scale Edo-period Osaka street rebuilt indoors — rent a kimono and walk it.
  • 🖼Nakanoshima Museum of Art
    Nakanoshima, Osaka · approx. ¥1,200 · 90 min
    A black cube on the river holding Modigliani and a deep collection of Osaka modernism.
  • 🏯Osaka Castle Main Tower
    Osaka Castle Park · approx. ¥600 · 90 min
    Inside the keep: Hideyoshi's story over eight floors, ending on an open-air deck.
  • 🎤Namba Grand Kagetsu Comedy Theatre
    Namba, Osaka · approx. ¥4,800 · 120 min
    The home stage of Osaka stand-up (manzai). Fast Japanese, but the crowd energy carries it.
  • ⚛Osaka Science Museum
    Nakanoshima, Osaka · approx. ¥600 · 120 min
    Hands-on physics floors plus one of Japan's larger planetarium domes.
  • 🍢Dotonbori Night Food Crawl
    Dotonbori, Osaka · approx. ¥9,000 · 180 min
    Five or six stops a local would pick — kushikatsu, takoyaki, standing bars.
  • 🥩Wagyu Teppanyaki Counter Dinner
    Shinsaibashi, Osaka · approx. ¥15,000 · 120 min
    A5 beef cooked in front of you at the counter, with the chef explaining each cut.
  • 🥷Ninja Experience & Shuriken Throwing
    Namba, Osaka · approx. ¥4,000 · 90 min
    Costume, blowgun and shuriken, taught with more discipline than you'd expect.
  • 🗡Sword-Smithing & Blade Polishing Demo
    Osaka city · approx. ¥9,000 · 120 min
    Watch folding and quenching up close, then try the polishing stones on a practice blade.
  • 🥘Okonomiyaki & Kushikatsu Class
    Namba, Osaka · approx. ¥5,500 · 120 min
    Cook both Osaka staples on your own hotplate, and learn the no-double-dipping rule.
  • 🪡Sashiko Stitching Workshop
    Osaka city · approx. ¥4,000 · 120 min
    The running-stitch geometry once used to mend workwear, on a coaster or small cloth.
  • 🔪Sakai Knife Sharpening & Forging Visit
    Sakai, Osaka · approx. ¥7,500 · 120 min
    Where most Japanese chefs' knives are made — sharpen one on a whetstone and buy at source.
  • 💠Osaka Digital Art Museum
    Osaka bayfront · approx. ¥4,800 · 120 min
    Projection-mapped rooms you walk through barefoot — a reliable rainy-afternoon fix.
  • 👘Kimono Rental & Photo Walk (Osaka)
    Namba / Osaka Castle · approx. ¥4,000 · 180 min
    Dressing included, hair styling optional, and you keep it on for the rest of the day.

Mostly covered

  • 🦀Kuromon Market Food Tour
    Nipponbashi, Osaka · approx. ¥8,300 · 150 min
    Osaka's kitchen with a guide — crab, uni, wagyu skewers and takoyaki, all under the arcade roof.

Best under blue sky

  • 🎡Tempozan Giant Ferris Wheel
    Tempozan, Osaka · approx. ¥900 · 30 min
    One of the world's tallest Ferris wheels — bay, city and Akashi Bridge views by night.
  • 🗼Tower of the Sun (Expo '70 Park)
    Suita, Osaka · approx. ¥1,200 · 90 min
    Okamoto Taro's giant 1970 monument — book ahead to see the restored interior.
  • 🌳Expo '70 Commemorative Park
    Suita, Osaka · approx. ¥260 · 180 min
    Vast park around the Tower of the Sun — Japanese garden, museums and seasonal flowers.
  • 🎮Universal Studios Japan 1-Day
    Konohana, Osaka · approx. ¥10,400 · 480 min
    Super Nintendo World and wizardry — Osaka's blockbuster park.
  • 🌇Umeda Sky Observatory
    Umeda, Osaka · approx. ¥1,400 · 60 min
    A floating glass escalator to 360° city views.
  • 🚤Dotonbori River Cruise
    Dotonbori, Osaka · approx. ¥1,100 · 30 min
    20 neon-soaked minutes on the Dotonbori canal.
  • 🚌Arashiyama · Nara · Fushimi Inari Day Loop
    Departs Osaka / Kyoto · approx. ¥8,200 · 600 min
    Three Kansai icons in one coach day — bamboo groves, bowing deer and endless vermilion torii gates.
  • 🦒Tennoji Zoo
    Tennoji, Osaka · approx. ¥500 · 150 min
    A century-old city zoo in the park below Tsutenkaku — hippos, lions and a bird aviary.
  • 🎢Hirakata Park
    Hirakata, Osaka · approx. ¥5,300 · 300 min
    Kansai's oldest amusement park — a wooden coaster, a pool in summer and a rose garden.
  • ⛴Osaka Aqua-Liner River Cruise
    Osakajo Pier · approx. ¥1,800 · 60 min
    A low-slung boat that ducks under the old bridges between the castle and Nakanoshima.
  • ⛰Mozu Tombs & Daisen Kofun Museum
    Sakai, Osaka · approx. ¥300 · 90 min
    A World Heritage keyhole tomb bigger than the Pyramids' base — best grasped from the museum.
  • 🏯Kishiwada Castle & Hachijin Garden
    Kishiwada, Osaka · approx. ¥700 · 70 min
    A rebuilt keep over a modernist stone garden laid out like a battle formation.
  • 🍄Super Nintendo World (USJ Express)
    Universal Studios Japan · approx. ¥8,600 · 300 min
    Mario Kart in augmented reality, and a Power-Up Band that makes the whole area interactive.
