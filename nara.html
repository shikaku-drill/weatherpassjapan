What to do in Nara when it rains

Fine in the rain — indoor

  • 🗿Nara National Museum
    Nara Park, Nara · pay at the gate · 75 min
    Japan's finest Buddhist sculpture hall, right by the deer — a perfect rainy-day stop in Nara.
  • 🍶Nara Sake Brewery Tasting
    Naramachi, Nara · pay at the gate · 90 min
    Nara is where sake brewing began — taste flights in a centuries-old machiya brewery, rain or shine.
  • 🍣Kakinoha-zushi (Persimmon-Leaf Sushi) Class
    Nara City · approx. ¥4,500 · 90 min
    Wrap salted mackerel and rice in persimmon leaves — Nara's mountain-village preserve.

Best under blue sky

  • 🦌Nara Deer Park & Todai-ji Tour
    Nara Park · approx. ¥7,200 · 300 min
    Feed the famous bowing deer and see the Great Buddha.
  • 👘Nara Kimono Rental & Park Stroll
    Naramachi, Nara · approx. ¥3,400 · 240 min
    Slip into a kimono and walk to the deer, Todai-ji and Naramachi's old lanes — return by evening.
  • 🛕Horyu-ji Temple
    Ikaruga, Nara · pay at the gate · 120 min
    The world's oldest wooden buildings, standing since the 7th century — Japan's first World Heritage site.
  • 🌸Mt. Yoshino & Asuka Day Tour
    Departs Nara · approx. ¥5,600 · 420 min
    Japan's most famous cherry mountain plus the ancient capital of Asuka — deep Nara beyond the deer.
  • 🛕Yakushi-ji Temple
    Nishinokyo, Nara · approx. ¥1,600 · 70 min
    A World Heritage temple whose east pagoda has stood since the 8th century.
  • 🛕Toshodai-ji Temple
    Nishinokyo, Nara · approx. ¥1,000 · 60 min
    Founded by the Chinese monk Ganjin — the Golden Hall is Nara-period architecture at its calmest.
  • 🏯Heijo Palace Site & Museum
    Nara City · approx. ¥500 · 90 min
    The vast 8th-century capital, with a reconstructed audience hall on open grassland.
