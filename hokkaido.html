What to do in Hokkaido when it rains

22 places across Hokkaido, with 7 that work in the rain. Pick by the weather you actually have — the live app sorts them by how long it takes to get there from where you stand.

Fine in the rain — indoor

  • 🍪Shiroi Koibito Park
    Miyanosawa, Sapporo · approx. ¥750 · 90 min
    Watch Hokkaido's iconic cookies being made, then bake your own.
  • 🍺Sapporo Beer Museum
    Sapporo Garden Park · approx. ¥1,000 · 60 min
    Japan's only beer museum, in a red-brick brewery — tasting flight at the end.
  • 🔮Otaru Glass & Music Box Workshop
    Otaru, Hokkaido · approx. ¥3,000 · 90 min
    Blow your own glass or assemble a music box in Otaru's historic canal district — a cozy indoor craft.
  • 🦀Hokkaido Crab & Seafood Dinner
    Susukino, Sapporo · approx. ¥12,000 · 120 min
    Three kinds of crab, scallop and uni — the reason people fly to Sapporo in winter.
  • 🦑Squid Fishing & Sashimi at Hakodate Market
    Hakodate, Hokkaido · approx. ¥3,000 · 60 min
    Catch a live squid from the market tank and have it sliced in front of you, still moving.

Mostly covered

  • ♨️Noboribetsu Onsen & Hell Valley
    Noboribetsu, Hokkaido · approx. ¥2,700 · 180 min
    Steaming crater walks then Hokkaido's most famous hot springs — an easy trip south of Sapporo.
  • 🦑Hakodate Morning Market
    Hakodate, Hokkaido · pay at the gate · 60 min
    Catch-your-own squid and fresh uni-crab rice bowls at dawn by the harbour.

Best under blue sky

  • 🐧Asahiyama Zoo Day Tour (from Sapporo)
    Departs Sapporo · approx. ¥9,500 · 600 min
    Japan's most famous zoo — watch penguins waddle past on a winter walk and see animals in action-first enclosures.
  • 🏞Blue Pond & Shirahige Falls Day Tour
    Departs Sapporo / Asahikawa · approx. ¥7,900 · 540 min
    The surreal cobalt-blue pond made famous as an Apple wallpaper, plus the milky Shirahige waterfall.
  • 🎿Niseko Ski & Snow Experience
    Niseko, Hokkaido · approx. ¥6,200 · 360 min
    World-famous powder snow with lessons and gear included — Japan's top winter resort for first-timers and pros.
  • 🌃Mt. Hakodate Ropeway Night View
    Hakodate, Hokkaido · approx. ¥1,700 · 60 min
    The glittering hourglass of Hakodate at night — one of the world's celebrated cityscapes.
  • 💜Farm Tomita (Furano Lavender)
    Nakafurano, Hokkaido · pay at the gate · 90 min
    Rainbow flower fields and free lavender-lined terraces — Hokkaido's summer icon, no ticket needed.
  • ⛷Sapporo Half-Day Ski Lesson & Rental
    Sapporo Teine / Bankei · approx. ¥9,000 · 420 min
    Powder within an hour of the city, gear and an English instructor included.
