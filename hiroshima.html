What to do in Hiroshima when it rains

17 places across Hiroshima, with 6 that work in the rain. Pick by the weather you actually have — the live app sorts them by how long it takes to get there from where you stand.

Fine in the rain — indoor

  • 🥞Hiroshima Okonomiyaki Class
    Central Hiroshima · approx. ¥3,800 · 90 min
    Layer noodles, cabbage and batter Hiroshima-style, coached by a local pro.
  • 🕊️Peace Memorial Museum & Guide
    Central Hiroshima · approx. ¥2,400 · 120 min
    A moving, essential museum — with an English-speaking guide.
  • 🚗Mazda Museum Factory Tour
    Fuchu, Hiroshima · pay at the gate · 90 min
    Rotary-engine history and a working assembly line — a free, reservation-only look inside a car giant.
  • ♨Dogo Onsen Honkan Bath
    Matsuyama, Ehime · approx. ¥1,000 · 120 min
    The 1894 wooden bathhouse said to have inspired Spirited Away. Soap not included — bring a towel.
  • 🦪Miyajima Oyster & Sake Tasting
    Miyajima, Hiroshima · approx. ¥6,000 · 90 min
    Grilled and raw oysters from the bay that grows most of Japan's, with Hiroshima sake.
  • 🍶Saijo Sake Brewery Street
    Saijo, Higashihiroshima · pay at the gate · 120 min
    One of Japan's three great sake towns — red-brick chimneys, white walls and tasting flights indoors.

Best under blue sky

  • ⛩Miyajima & Floating Torii Tour
    Miyajima, Hiroshima · approx. ¥9,000 · 420 min
    Ferry to the floating torii — timed with the tide.
  • 🕊Hiroshima Peace Park & Miyajima Full-Day
    Departs Hiroshima / Osaka · approx. ¥10,200 · 600 min
    The Peace Memorial and A-Bomb Dome paired with Miyajima's floating torii — Hiroshima's two icons in one guided day.
  • 🚴Shimanami Kaido Island Cycling (bike hire)
    Onomichi → Imabari · approx. ¥9,000 · 480 min
    70km of bridges island-hopping the Seto Inland Sea. One-way hire, drop off at the far end.
  • 🛶Miyajima Torii Sea Kayak
    Miyajima, Hiroshima · approx. ¥7,500 · 150 min
    Paddle up to the floating gate at high tide — the view the ferries can't give you.
  • 🚲Onomichi & Shimanami Cycling
    Onomichi, Hiroshima · approx. ¥4,700 · 300 min
    Ride bridge-hopping over the Inland Sea from a temple-strewn hillside port — Japan's best cycle route.
  • ⛵Tomonoura Port Town
    Fukuyama, Hiroshima · pay at the gate · 150 min
    An Edo-era harbour with a stone lantern pier — the seaside town that inspired Ponyo.
