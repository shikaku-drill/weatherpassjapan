Fine in the rain — indoor

  • ✈Museum of Aeronautical Sciences
    Narita Airport area · approx. ¥700 · 120 min
    Right under the Narita flight path — cockpit simulators and a 747 nose section. Handy on a layover.
  • 🖼Hoki Museum
    Chiba City (Toke) · approx. ¥1,830 · 90 min
    A cantilevered steel tube of a building, filled with Japanese photo-realist painting.
  • 🎨DIC Kawamura Memorial Museum of Art
    Sakura, Chiba · approx. ¥1,500 · 120 min
    Rothko's seven-panel room, plus Monet and Renoir, in gardens 30 minutes from Narita.
  • 🏛National Museum of Japanese History
    Sakura, Chiba · approx. ¥600 · 150 min
    All of Japanese history in six halls — the best single overview in the country, near Narita.
  • 🐠Inage Aquarium Park (Chiba Port)
    Chiba Port · approx. ¥700 · 90 min
    A glass tower over the bay — sunset views across the industrial waterfront to Fuji.

Best under blue sky

  • 🏰Tokyo Disney Resort 1-Day Passport
    Maihama, Chiba · approx. ¥10,700 · 480 min
    The classic — parades, castles and queues; check the sky first.
  • 🐋Kamogawa Sea World
    Kamogawa, Chiba · approx. ¥3,200 · 180 min
    Killer-whale performances by the Pacific — Chiba's biggest marine park, easy from Tokyo.
  • ⛩Naritasan Shinshoji Temple
    Narita, Chiba · pay at the gate · 150 min
    A 1,000-year-old temple and its eel-lined approach — a classic first or last stop near the airport.
  • 🐑Mother Farm
    Boso Peninsula, Chiba · approx. ¥1,700 · 240 min
    Hillside farm with sheep shows, flower fields and fruit picking — a family day out from Tokyo.
  • 🌷Tokyo German Village
    Sodegaura, Chiba · approx. ¥1,100 · 150 min
    Flower fields by day and one of Kanto's biggest winter illuminations by night, across Tokyo Bay.
  • ⛰Mt. Nokogiri Ropeway & Nihon-ji
    Boso, Chiba · pay at the gate · 120 min
    A ropeway to a cliff temple with the 'Hell Peek' overhang and a giant stone Buddha over the bay.
  • 🚣Sawara Little-Edo Boat Ride
    Sawara, Chiba · pay at the gate · 60 min
    Drift a willow-lined canal through a preserved Edo merchant town — 'Little Edo' near Narita.
