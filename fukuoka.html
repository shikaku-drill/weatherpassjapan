What to do in Fukuoka when it rains

18 places across Fukuoka, with 7 that work in the rain. Pick by the weather you actually have — the live app sorts them by how long it takes to get there from where you stand.

Fine in the rain — indoor

  • 🌿teamLab Forest Fukuoka
    E·ZO Fukuoka, Hakata Bay · approx. ¥2,300 · 90 min
    A glowing digital forest you catch creatures in — next to the baseball dome.
  • 🍜Tonkotsu Ramen Cooking Class
    Hakata, Fukuoka · approx. ¥6,500 · 120 min
    Learn the pork-bone broth in the city that invented it, then eat two bowls.
  • 🧵Hakata-ori Weaving Workshop
    Hakata, Fukuoka · approx. ¥5,500 · 100 min
    Weave the thick silk obi cloth that samurai once used to hold their swords in place.
  • 🎎Hakata Doll Painting Workshop
    Hakata, Fukuoka · pay at the gate · 90 min
    Paint a 400-year-old clay-doll craft with a Hakata artisan — indoors, and yours to take home.

Mostly covered

  • 🏮Hakata Yatai Food Stalls
    Nakasu, Fukuoka · pay at the gate · 150 min
    Bar-hop Fukuoka's riverside food stalls like a local.
  • 🗼Fukuoka Tower Observatory
    Momochi, Fukuoka · approx. ¥700 · 60 min
    Seaside mirror-glass tower with 360° bay views — best at sunset and after dark.
  • 🍢Nakasu Yatai Food-Stall Tour
    Nakasu, Fukuoka · approx. ¥7,000 · 120 min
    Riverside stalls with a guide to order for you — the reason Fukuoka eats outdoors.

Best under blue sky

  • 🌷Huis Ten Bosch (Nagasaki)
    Sasebo, Nagasaki · approx. ¥6,700 · 480 min
    A Dutch-themed resort of canals, tulips and Europe's grandest illumination — a huge day out from Fukuoka.
  • ⛩Dazaifu Shrine & Yanagawa Day Tour
    Departs Fukuoka · approx. ¥6,500 · 480 min
    The shrine to Japan's god of learning, plus a gondola glide through the willow-lined canals of Yanagawa.
  • ♨️Beppu & Yufuin Onsen Day Tour
    Departs Fukuoka · approx. ¥8,500 · 600 min
    The steaming 'hells' of Beppu and the arty hot-spring town of Yufuin — Kyushu's onsen heartland in a day.
  • ♨Beppu Hells & Sand Bath
    Beppu, Oita · approx. ¥2,200 · 180 min
    Cobalt and blood-red springs too hot to enter, then be buried in hot sand by the sea.
  • 🛕Nanzoin Reclining Buddha
    Sasaguri, Fukuoka · pay at the gate · 90 min
    The world's largest bronze reclining Buddha — 41 metres long, in a green valley near Hakata.
  • 🚢Mojiko Retro Harbour
    Kitakyushu, Fukuoka · pay at the gate · 120 min
    Taisho-era brick warehouses on the strait, with a rooftop view across to Shimonoseki.
