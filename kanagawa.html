What to do in Kanagawa when it rains

37 places across Kanagawa, with 10 that work in the rain. Pick by the weather you actually have — the live app sorts them by how long it takes to get there from where you stand.

Fine in the rain — indoor

  • 🍥Cup Noodles Museum Yokohama
    Minato Mirai, Yokohama · approx. ¥500 · 90 min
    Design your own cup and invent your own noodle flavor to take home.
  • 🍜Shin-Yokohama Ramen Museum
    Shin-Yokohama, Kanagawa · pay at the gate · 90 min
    A retro-Showa alley of regional ramen shops — taste your way across Japan, fully indoors.
  • 🖼Pola Museum of Art (Hakone)
    Sengokuhara, Hakone · approx. ¥1,800 · 120 min
    Monet, Renoir and Picasso buried in a forest — the rainy-day answer in Hakone.
  • 💎Lalique Museum Hakone
    Sengokuhara, Hakone · approx. ¥1,500 · 100 min
    Art-nouveau glass, and tea inside a restored Orient Express carriage.
  • 🏺Okada Museum of Art
    Kowakudani, Hakone · approx. ¥2,800 · 150 min
    Five floors of Japanese and Chinese art, with a hot-spring foot bath at the entrance.
  • 🐬Yokohama Hakkeijima Sea Paradise
    Hakkeijima, Yokohama · approx. ¥5,600 · 240 min
    Four aquariums plus rides on an island — a full rainy day for families.
  • 🚂Hara Model Railway Museum
    Yokohama Station area · approx. ¥1,200 · 90 min
    The world's largest indoor model railway, running on real rails with real sound.
  • ♨Hakone Yuryo Day Onsen
    Hakone-Yumoto · approx. ¥1,500 · 180 min
    Forest bathhouse five minutes from Yumoto station — private open-air tubs bookable.

Mostly covered

  • ♨️Hakone Kowakien Yunessun
    Hakone, Kanagawa · approx. ¥3,500 · 180 min
    A hot-spring water park — swimsuit baths, wine and coffee pools, plus traditional onsen.
  • 🔮Hakone Venetian Glass Museum
    Sengokuhara, Hakone · approx. ¥1,800 · 90 min
    Venetian glass indoors, and a crystal arch in the garden that glitters even in drizzle.

Best under blue sky

  • ⛴️Hakone Ropeway & Cruise Pass
    Hakone, Kanagawa · approx. ¥6,300 · 360 min
    Ropeway over volcanic valleys, pirate ship across Lake Ashi.
  • 🌊Kamakura & Enoshima Day Tour
    Departs Tokyo · approx. ¥9,500 · 540 min
    The Great Buddha, the Enoden coastal train and Enoshima's sea views — the classic seaside escape from Tokyo.
  • 🗿Hakone Open-Air Museum
    Hakone, Kanagawa · approx. ¥1,700 · 120 min
    Sculptures across mountain lawns and a Picasso hall — art in the open, foot-bath included.
  • 🌅Enoshima Sea Candle & Iwaya Caves
    Enoshima, Kanagawa · approx. ¥1,000 · 120 min
    A lighthouse-observatory with Fuji sunsets and sea caves — an easy island escape near Kamakura.
  • 🍁Sankeien Garden
    Yokohama, Kanagawa · pay at the gate · 90 min
    A classical strolling garden with relocated historic buildings and a three-storey pagoda.
  • 🏴‍☠️Hakone Lake Cruise & Ropeway
    Lake Ashi, Hakone · approx. ¥2,400 · 120 min
    A pirate-ship cruise across Lake Ashi to the Hakone shrine torii, then a ropeway over the valley.
  • 🎋Hokoku-ji Bamboo Temple
    Kamakura, Kanagawa · pay at the gate · 45 min
    Two thousand moso bamboo stems around a tea house. Quieter than Arashiyama, and you can sit with matcha inside it.
  • 🦏Yokohama Zoorasia
    Asahi, Yokohama · approx. ¥800 · 180 min
    Animals grouped by world climate zone across a huge wooded site — okapi included.
  • 🏯Odawara Castle
    Odawara, Kanagawa · approx. ¥510 · 90 min
    The Hojo clan's stronghold, on the way to Hakone — ninja museum for kids next door.
  • 🚲Lake Ashi E-Bike Tour
    Lake Ashi, Hakone · approx. ¥7,000 · 210 min
    Electric assist makes the Hakone hills manageable — cedar avenue, shrine gate, lake shore.
