Fine in the rain — indoor

  • ✨Gold Leaf Craft Experience
    Higashi Chaya, Kanazawa · approx. ¥1,700 · 60 min
    Gild your own chopsticks or box with real Kanazawa gold leaf.
  • 🏊21st Century Museum of Art
    Central Kanazawa · approx. ¥1,150 · 90 min
    Home of the famous 'Swimming Pool' — stand underwater without getting wet.
  • 🏯Nomura Samurai House
    Nagamachi, Kanazawa · pay at the gate · 60 min
    A preserved samurai residence with a jewel-box garden in Kanazawa's old warrior quarter.
  • 🍶Kanazawa Gold-Leaf & Sake Pairing
    Higashi Chaya, Kanazawa · approx. ¥5,500 · 120 min
    Apply gold leaf to a lacquer dish, then drink sake with gold flakes in it.
  • 🍣Kanazawa Sushi Counter Omakase
    Kanazawa · approx. ¥9,000 · 120 min
    Sea of Japan fish — sweet shrimp, gasu-ebi and buri — at a counter far cheaper than Tokyo.
  • 🍵Kanazawa Tea Ceremony (Chaya House)
    Higashi Chaya, Kanazawa · pay at the gate · 60 min
    Matcha and gold-leaf sweets in a preserved geisha teahouse — indoors, and deeply Kanazawa.
  • 🎨Kaga Yuzen Silk Dyeing Workshop
    Kanazawa, Ishikawa · pay at the gate · 90 min
    Hand-paint Kanazawa's famous silk dye in a riverside studio — take your work home, rain or shine.

Mostly covered

  • 🍣Omicho Market Sushi & Seafood
    Omicho, Kanazawa · pay at the gate · 90 min
    Sea-of-Japan snow crab, sweet shrimp and buri over rice in Kanazawa's covered market.

Best under blue sky

  • 🏘Shirakawa-go & Takayama Day Tour
    Departs Nagoya / Kanazawa · approx. ¥9,200 · 600 min
    The UNESCO thatched-roof village of Shirakawa-go, paired with Takayama's old merchant streets — a storybook day.
