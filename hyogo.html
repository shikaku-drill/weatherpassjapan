Fine in the rain — indoor

  • ♨️Arima Onsen Day Bath Pass
    Kobe, Hyogo · approx. ¥3,600 · 180 min
    Japan's oldest hot-spring town — soak in iron-rich 'gold' and clear 'silver' waters, an easy trip from Osaka.
  • 🗼Kobe Port Tower
    Meriken Park, Kobe · approx. ¥1,200 · 60 min
    The red lattice tower on the waterfront, with a rooftop deck over the harbour lights.
  • 🏛Kitano Ijinkan Western Houses
    Kitano, Kobe · approx. ¥750 · 120 min
    Hillside mansions built by foreign merchants after the port opened — several open to visit.
  • 🍶Nada Sake Brewery District
    Nada, Kobe · approx. ¥500 · 90 min
    Japan's largest sake-producing district — museum breweries with tastings straight from the tank.
  • 🥯Kobe Anpanman Children's Museum
    Harborland, Kobe · approx. ¥2,000 · 150 min
    Indoor play world for small children, right beside the harbour mall.
  • 🐟Atoa Aquarium Kobe
    Shinko, Kobe · approx. ¥1,800 · 120 min
    An art-and-aquarium hybrid — tanks staged like installations across themed dark rooms.
  • ♨Kinosaki Onsen Seven-Bath Pass
    Toyooka, Hyogo · approx. ¥1,500 · 180 min
    Stroll the willow-lined canal in a yukata and hop between seven public bath houses.
  • 🪚Takenaka Carpentry Tools Museum
    Shin-Kobe · approx. ¥700 · 90 min
    Japan's only museum of carpentry tools — joinery you can touch, and a full tea-house frame.
  • 🌊Disaster Reduction and Human Renovation Institution
    HAT Kobe · approx. ¥600 · 120 min
    The 1995 earthquake told through survivor testimony — sobering, and very well done.
  • 🐬Kobe Suma Sea World
    Suma, Kobe · approx. ¥1,800 · 120 min
    Rebuilt beachfront aquarium — the only orca show in western Japan.
  • 🥩Kobe Beef Steak Dinner
    Sannomiya, Kobe · approx. ¥16,000 · 120 min
    Certified Kobe beef with the certificate on the wall — the real thing, at the source.
  • 🧸Arima Toy Museum
    Arima Onsen · approx. ¥900 · 90 min
    German wooden toys and tin robots over six floors — a rainy hour in the onsen town.
  • ♨Kinosaki Private Onsen & Yukata
    Toyooka, Hyogo · approx. ¥3,000 · 150 min
    A private tub and a rented yukata, so you can walk the canal between the public baths.
  • 🍶Nada Sake Brewing Class & Tasting
    Nada, Kobe · approx. ¥3,000 · 90 min
    Polish rice, smell the koji, taste unpasteurised sake straight from the tank.

Best under blue sky

  • 🌿Kobe Nunobiki Herb Garden & Ropeway
    Kobe, Hyogo · approx. ¥1,700 · 120 min
    Ride a ropeway to a hillside herb garden with sweeping bay views and a glasshouse to duck into if it rains.
  • 🏯Himeji Castle & Koko-en Garden Tour
    Departs Osaka · approx. ¥6,900 · 480 min
    Japan's finest surviving feudal castle — a brilliant-white 'White Heron' fortress and its samurai-era garden.
  • 🌉Akashi Kaikyo Bridge Sky Walk
    Maiko, Kobe · approx. ¥1,100 · 90 min
    Walk out on the world's longest suspension bridge, 47m above the strait on a glass floor.
  • 🏯Takeda Castle Ruins (Castle in the Sky)
    Asago, Hyogo · approx. ¥500 · 120 min
    Stone ramparts on a ridge that float above autumn morning mist — a stiff uphill walk.
  • 🌃Mt. Rokko & Maya Night View
    Kobe, Hyogo · approx. ¥2,300 · 150 min
    One of Japan's 'three great night views' — a 10-million-dollar carpet of Kobe city lights from the Kikuseidai deck.
  • 🐼Kobe Oji Zoo
    Nada, Kobe · approx. ¥600 · 150 min
    A hillside zoo with koalas and a preserved Western mansion on the grounds.
