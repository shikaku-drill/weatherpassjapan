What to do in Okinawa when it rains

16 places across Okinawa, with 7 that work in the rain. Pick by the weather you actually have — the live app sorts them by how long it takes to get there from where you stand.

Fine in the rain — indoor

  • 🫙Ryukyu Glass Blowing
    Itoman, Okinawa · approx. ¥2,000 · 45 min
    Blow your own ocean-colored glass cup with an Okinawan craftsman.
  • 🐠DMM Kariyushi Aquarium
    Tomigusuku, Okinawa · approx. ¥2,500 · 90 min
    A compact, high-tech aquarium ten minutes from Naha Airport.
  • 🐋Okinawa Churaumi Aquarium
    Motobu, Okinawa · approx. ¥1,900 · 180 min
    The legendary Kuroshio tank — whale sharks overhead.
  • 🎨teamLab Future Park Okinawa
    Omoromachi, Naha · approx. ¥2,300 · 90 min
    A kid-friendly digital playground inside DFS Naha — draw a fish and watch it swim the wall-sized aquarium.
  • 🫙Ryukyu Glass Blowing
    Naha, Okinawa · approx. ¥4,000 · 90 min
    Blow a tumbler in the thick, bubbled style born from reusing postwar bottles.
  • 🎨Bingata Stencil Dyeing (Okinawa)
    Naha, Okinawa · approx. ¥4,500 · 120 min
    Okinawa's royal dyeing tradition — bright hibiscus and fish stencilled onto cloth.

Mostly covered

  • 🏝Okinawa World & Gyokusendo Cave
    Nanjo, Okinawa · approx. ¥1,900 · 180 min
    A vast limestone cave plus Eisa drum shows and a snake park — all-weather south of Naha.

Best under blue sky

  • 🤿Blue Cave Snorkeling (Okinawa)
    Onna, Okinawa · approx. ¥5,200 · 180 min
    Snorkel into a sea cave glowing electric blue — Okinawa's signature marine experience, gear and guide included.
  • 🏝Kouri Island & Churaumi Coast Day Tour
    Departs Naha · approx. ¥6,500 · 540 min
    Cross the emerald sea bridge to Kouri Island and cruise Okinawa's north coast to the famed Churaumi shores.
  • 🪂Okinawa Parasailing & Marine Sports
    Naha, Okinawa · approx. ¥4,600 · 120 min
    Soar 40m above the East China Sea by parachute, then pick from jet-ski, banana boat and more off Naha.
  • 🛶Mangrove Kayak Tour
    Yanbaru, Okinawa · approx. ¥6,500 · 150 min
    Paddle the tidal creeks under mangrove roots — calm water, good for first-timers.
  • 🐋Whale-Watching Cruise (winter)
    Off Okinawa main island · approx. ¥6,000 · 180 min
    Humpbacks calve off Okinawa from January to March — sightings are the norm, not luck.
